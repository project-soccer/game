# ADR 0009: Use accessible football with bounded assisted touches

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-20
- Scope: Project Soccer
- Legacy references: D08, D11, D14, D38

## Context

The founder selected responsive but credible play, moderate assistance, and understandable errors. Pure free-body dribbling and rigid ball attachment each undermine that goal.

## Decision

Use a freely simulated ball between bounded guided touches. Pass/shot contact occurs at a scheduled reachable action phase. Sprint trades close control and turning for speed. Start the experiment with identical outfield attributes and no hidden random accuracy.

## Consequences

Assistance must not guarantee a recipient or prevent interception. Losing the ball during wind-up produces a missed attempt. Fine-tuning receiving, tackling, weak-foot effects, stamina, and contested possession requires playtests. Equal experimental attributes do not resolve NFT price/strength policy.

## Alternatives considered

Rigid foot attachment and unlimited-force player contacts were rejected. Full physical simulation of every limb is unnecessary for this experiment.
